# Pricing & competitive research

All prices live in [`public/pricing.js`](../public/pricing.js). The public quote page (`/quote`) and the server both use this one file, so changing a price there updates everything. The server recomputes every saved quote, which means a visitor can't alter prices in their browser.

> The prices below are **proposed defaults** based on the research in this document. They are not market data; set them to whatever fits your costs and positioning.

## What comparable services charge (published 2026 figures)

| Comparable | What they sell | Published pricing |
|---|---|---|
| AI automation agencies (US, small business) | Done-for-you workflow builds + retainers | Setup **$1,500–$5,000** for 1–2 workflows; retainers **$500–$3,500/mo**. Mid-market retainers run $4,000–$12,000/mo. |
| Lumo AI Agency (Austin) | Custom AI agents & automation | From **$1,200/mo** (Starter), $3,000/mo (Growth), $6,500/mo (Scale) |
| Independent AI consultants | Advice, training, builds | **$100–$350/hr**; boutique firms $125–$600/hr |
| AI training workshops | Small-team workshops | Half-day for up to 5 people **≈ $999**; full day ≈ $1,499 |
| Free alternatives | AI literacy workshops | SBA AI Deep Dive Workshops, SCORE and local SBDCs (no cost) |
| AI receptionist software (customer pays the vendor) | Phone answering | Typically **$50–$300/mo**. My AI Front Desk $99/mo, Rosie from $49/mo, Smith.ai ≈ $150–$300/mo. |
| Scheduling software (customer pays the vendor) | Online booking | Calendly free / $10 / $16 per seat per month. Square Appointments is free with Square payments, with paid tiers ≈ $29–$69 per location per month. |

**Caveats.** Most agency figures come from agency-written pricing guides that have a commercial interest, and some of them cite each other. Software prices change often, so check them with the vendor before each quote; the quote page says this to customers.

## How Bizzle prices (and why)

| Item | Price | Positioning |
|---|---|---|
| 1:1 training session (60 min, up to 3 people) | $150 | Low end of independent-consultant hourly rates |
| Training pack (4 sessions + cheat-sheets + 2-week check-in) | $500 | $125 per session; built so new habits stick |
| Team workshop (half-day on-site, up to 8 people) | $900 | Under the ≈$999 benchmark for 5 people, and serves more people |
| Done-with-you workflow, **starter** (booking, reviews, missed-call text-back) | $750 | Below the agency setup range |

| Each additional location | +$250 per workflow | Covers copying the setup per site |
| Bundles | 10% off 2 workflows, 15% off 3 or more | Rewards a broader engagement |
| Care, Essentials (up to 2 workflows) | $149/mo | Well under the $500/mo agency retainer floor |
| Care, Plus (up to 5 workflows, monthly check-in) | $349/mo | Still under the agency floor |
| Paying in full up front | 5% off | Optional |
| 3 equal monthly payments, no interest | quotes over $1,000 | Removes the up-front barrier |

### What makes customers comfortable

- **The same published prices for everyone.** The quote page shows the market comparison beside the customer's total.
- **They approve the plan before work starts, and approve the result before the final payment.** The default payment option is 50% at the start and 50% after approval.
- **Third-party software is never marked up.** It is listed separately as "paid directly to the vendor", with typical ranges.
- **Every change can be rolled back.**
- **Care plans are month-to-month.** Customers who don't take one own the work outright.
- **Every setup includes the same steps:** discovery, a written plan, a walkthrough, a 2-week pilot, one training session and 30 days of tune-ups.

**Review these before using them publicly:** the "approve before final payment" promise, the 30 days of tune-ups, the no-markup promise and the 1-business-day response time. They are commitments your business would be making.
